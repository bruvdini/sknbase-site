Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

SKNBASE ("we", "us") provides an AI-powered skincare recommendation app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account info: email address, and name/avatar if you provide them.
  • Skin profile: skin type, concerns, city, budget, and quiz answers you submit to generate a routine.
  • Photos: selfies you take for face-scan skin analysis. These are stored in a private, encrypted storage bucket accessible only to your account, and are used solely to generate your analysis and let you track visual progress over time.
  • Usage data: routine check-ins, streaks, and app interactions needed to power your progress tracking.
  • Purchase data: subscription status via our payments provider (RevenueCat) and the App Store / Play Store. We do not receive or store your card details.

How we use your information

  • To generate personalized skincare routines and product recommendations.
  • To analyze face-scan photos and show you visual skin progress over time.
  • To operate account features such as streaks, reminders, and premium access.
  • To improve the app and respond to support requests.

Third parties we work with

We use trusted service providers to operate the app: Supabase (database and secure file storage), Anthropic (Claude, to analyze skin photos and generate routine text), Replicate (to remove photo backgrounds for your scan images), and RevenueCat (to manage subscriptions). These providers process data only as needed to deliver the app's features and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data — profile, routines, check-ins, and photos — at any time from Profile → Account → Delete Account. This action is irreversible.

Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at hello@sknbase.app for any request we can't handle directly in the app.

Not medical advice

SKNBASE provides general skincare guidance based on visible, non-medical observations. It is not a substitute for professional dermatological or medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Children's privacy

SKNBASE is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@sknbase.app.
